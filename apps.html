Example — Brighton Lawn + Landscape

BUILT AROUND HOW A
LANDSCAPING COMPANY RUNS

Brighton is a custom app we made for a real landscaping and snow company. The core is simple: schedule your workers and run the whole day from one owner dashboard.

Owner dashboard

An owner dashboard that tells you everything

Today's jobs, who is working, what needs your attention and photos to review, the moment you open the app.

Schedule workers

Schedule a worker in seconds

Pick the person, the job and the time. They get a notification, and conflicts are caught for you.

Live schedule

See every crew, every job, live

Weekly routes and one-off jobs, assigned to crews, with live status as the day moves.

And More

EVERYTHING ELSE THEY
NEEDED, ADDED IN

A time clock in every pocket

Crew clock in and out from the job site. You see hours as they happen, with no paper timesheets.

Projects with photos and tasks

Before-and-after photos, task checklists and crew scheduling for every build, in one place.

A simple customer view

Customers see the next visit, fresh photos of the work and their service history, and can ask for more.

Snow Mode for storm days

Dispatch routes, track every stop with photos, handle skip requests and tell customers when you've been.

A bonus: a public forecast

A small extra for customers: a clean forecast for any US town, with your own storm outlook on top.

Screens shown use made-up demo data.
